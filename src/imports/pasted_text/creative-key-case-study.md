2. THE CHALLENGE
-----------------------------------

Heading:
Creative opportunities existed. Finding a confident way in was harder.

Copy:
The Creative Futures Academy gives students access to specialist creative spaces, technologies and expertise. Our research showed that the challenge was not simply whether these opportunities existed.

Students could know that a space was there while still being unsure what happened inside, whether the technology was relevant to them, how to begin, or whether they were experienced enough to participate.

Design challenge:

“How might we support creative students in confidently discovering, understanding and integrating creative technologies into their creative practice?”

4. KEY INSIGHTS
-----------------------------------

Heading:
Three patterns shaped the direction

Create three large insight cards.

01 — DISCOVERY IS SOCIALLY DISTRIBUTED
Students often learn about creative opportunities through friends, staff and informal encounters rather than formal information alone.

Design implication:
Make opportunities visible while showing what people can actually create with them.

02 — CONFIDENCE GROWS THROUGH INVITATION
Awareness does not automatically create confidence. Demonstration, reassurance and supported first attempts matter.

Design implication:
Help users move from “What is this?” to “I can try this.”

03 — ACCESS ALSO HAS OPERATIONAL LIMITS
More visibility can create more demand. Equipment safety, staffing, training and responsibility therefore need to be considered alongside accessibility.

Design implication:
Design access as a supported service rather than unlimited self-service.

-----------------------------------

Heading:
The experience should continue — not restart.

Show kiosk flow and mobile screens side by side.

Explain:

I treated the kiosk, Creative Key and mobile companion as one service journey rather than three separate products.

The kiosk supports public discovery.

The Creative Key provides continuity.

The mobile companion handles longer, personalised and private interactions.

This also reduced the need to enter personal information on a public kiosk.

Permanent QR handoff

Scan-first rather than automatic printing

Print-on-demand Creative Key

Greater attention to maintenance, reach and physical servicing

One of my biggest lessons was that implementation is not separate from UX. Maintenance, staffing and physical constraints are part of the user experience.
